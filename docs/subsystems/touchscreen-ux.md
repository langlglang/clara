# Touchscreen UX handoff — proposed

The first screen appears on lid open and shows **Clara Demo**, a large **Mock SOS** action, the fictional box location, and a local scenario option. No login or network spinner covers these controls. The screen should work with one hand and support large targets, strong contrast, adjustable text, captions, spoken replay, and one step at a time.

During guidance, show the current versioned demo step, one matching illustration, the exact kit slot only when verified, and clear Next/Back/Repeat/Stop controls. A proposed LED beside that slot lights as a redundant physical cue; the screen still names and outlines the slot if the LED is unavailable. Audio and text share a step ID; interruption stops speech immediately and clears the old slot light. Camera preview and capture have explicit consent indicators. A cloud drop leaves the current step visible and offers the local scenario. Show “not verified” for uncertain item locations and “unavailable” for missing readings. A live sensor result shows value, unit, device and time without a per-reading experimental badge.

Add **Show handoff** as a secondary action during a scenario and after Mock SOS. The card names the fictional site, separates user reports from camera/sensor observations, lists steps shown versus acknowledged, and calls out open questions. Keep source and time readable; exclude volunteer observations without separate sharing consent. It must be available from local session events when offline and must never obscure Mock SOS. The companion preview is also marked simulated; no screen says a real responder was notified.

On lid open, the proposed open chime plays and stops at the first touch, scenario start or Mock SOS; the open screen carries a small line: "Camera took one photo when the box opened." At lid close with no emergency, ask "Was anything taken from the box?" with the tray diagram. See [anti-theft](anti-theft.md).

For the [box network](box-network.md): when another box's Mock SOS opens, an idle box shows a full-screen card ("Emergency at Room 101. Bring this kit.") with written directions and a large **Bringing this kit** button; a box in use shows a banner instead and keeps its guide and Mock SOS. After Mock SOS, the originating box shows whether nearby boxes were notified, and any **Bringing this kit** taps as unconfirmed. When an item is missing, a **Nearest other kit** card shows the location, directions and how old that box's status is.

Test on the exact HDMI/USB display and enclosure. Acceptance: mock SOS reachable within one interaction from every screen; visible offline and simulation states; no stale visual after step change; accessible with mic/camera disabled; user can follow an offline scenario and show a factual handoff card without agent assistance.
